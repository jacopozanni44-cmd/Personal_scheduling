Events

Events found in your images will show up here. Check them, then press "Add to Outlook".

With Outlook connected, "Add to calendar" creates the event straight in your default calendar. "Open in Outlook" instead opens Outlook on the web with the event filled in (pick the account type at the top), and you press Save there. All-day events always use "Open in Outlook". Times use your device's time zone ().

Solid = in your Outlook calendar. Dashed = found in a screenshot but not added yet.
